From the Ashes

Rebuild a self, a rhythm and a creative life on the other side of gambling. A three-month private programme, for the rebuild — not the emergency.

Application only. I take just a handful of people at a time.

Stopping is not the same as recovering. Most people who leave a compulsion behind are left standing in the wreckage of the life it built, facing a harder question than “how do I stop?” — “who am I now, and what is my life even for?”

From the Ashes is built for that question. It is for the rebuild: constructing a new identity, a steadier daily rhythm, and a creative practice that makes the old life genuinely uninteresting. It is led by someone who has stood on both sides of this — who spent twenty-five years inside harmful habits, lost almost everything, and built his way back through making, not merely abstaining.

Who this is for

This is for you if…

  • You have stopped, or largely stopped — but you feel adrift, as though the compulsion is gone and so is the person you used to be.
  • You are high-functioning and private, and you want depth and discretion, not a group room.
  • You are ready to build something — not only to talk about the past.
An honest word on who it isn’t for This is coaching, not treatment, and it begins where the emergency ends. If you’re in acute crisis or active financial harm, you deserve immediate specialist support first — the Other Support page lists it, and I’ll gladly point you there. This programme will still be here when you’re ready.
The journey

Three phases. Twelve weeks. One rebuild.

The shape follows my own way back — and the structure of the book it takes its name from.

1

The Fire

Weeks 1–4 — the honest reckoning. We map what the compulsion was really giving you — not greed, but aliveness — and steady the foundations: sleep, movement, breath. You leave understanding the machine that ran you.

2

The Ash

Weeks 5–8 — clear the ground. We rebuild a daily rhythm and begin a real creative practice — something made with your hands. Not art therapy; identity relocation through making. You leave with a practice, and the first thing you have built.

3

From the Ashes

Weeks 9–12 — make something, become someone. We deepen the practice into identity and purpose, and design the life you will actually live. You leave with a finished piece — proof, in your own hands, that you create rather than destroy.
