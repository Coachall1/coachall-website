From the Ashes

An application to work together.

Thank you for considering this. From the Ashes is a small, private programme — I take only a handful of people at a time, and I read every application myself. There are no wrong answers; it’s simply how we both find out whether this is the right work at the right time.

Before you begin — is now the right time?

This is coaching, not treatment, and it’s built for the rebuild after the worst of it — not the emergency itself. If either of these is true for you right now, please reach out to the support below first. It’s the braver, better step, and this programme will still be here when you’re ready.

  • You’re in financial crisis, or still gambling in a way that is actively harming you or the people around you.
  • You’re having thoughts of harming yourself, or you don’t feel safe.

Immediate, free, confidential support (UK):

  • National Gambling Helpline (GamCare) — 0808 8020 133 — free, 24 hours a day, every day.
  • Samaritans — 116 123 — any time, about anything.
  • In an emergency — call 999.
